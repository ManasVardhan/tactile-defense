# Unit Squads Plan 1 (Controls) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** In an attack battle the player deploys 3-soldier units onto hexes by clicking, selects and moves each unit independently, and pans the camera; playable on a designed tutorial Level 1 with Attackers and a Tank in a web build (spec checkpoint 1).

**Architecture:** A new `TactileWars.Army` layer (hex grid, `PlayerUnit`, `PlayerArmy`, `ArmyInput`) owns the player's forces in attack battles and reuses `Soldier`, `SoldierMotor`, `NavGrid`, `Weapon`, `Health`. `BattleManager`, `BattleCamera` and the HUD talk to an `IPlayerForce` interface that both `PlayerArmy` and the legacy `SquadController` implement. Defense battles keep `SquadController` and `AttackerAI` untouched; the Battle scene holds both and `BattleSetup` enables one by mode.

**Tech Stack:** Unity 6000.3.22f1, URP, C#, Unity Test Framework (EditMode and PlayMode), Input System via `Core/PointerInput`.

**Spec:** `Docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-unit-squads-hex-deploy-design.md`. This plan covers spec sections 5.1 to 5.4, the Attacker and Tank rows of 5.5, health pips, 5.7 (except mercenary and research changes) and the delivery note in 11. It also covers Level 1 of 6.1 and the tutorial in 6.2. Plan 2 covers the other four unit types and designed Levels 2 to 6. Plan 3 covers the ladder (6), squad builder (7), mercenary, research cleanup and Levels 7 to 10.

## Review Focus

1. A click outside the map, or on a blocked or occupied hex, while a button is armed: nothing deploys, the entry stays Ready, a reject cue plays, no exception. (Task 5)
2. A move order to a hex that no path reaches (walled off): rejected, the unit stays put, and the destination is not left reserved. (Task 3)
3. A unit destroyed while walking: its reserved destination hex becomes free again. (Task 3)
4. A press that starts on the map and drags past the tap threshold pans the camera and neither selects, deploys nor moves; a press that starts over HUD does nothing on the map. (Task 5)
5. After the battle ends (win or loss), clicks and the unit bar do nothing and the armed state clears. (Task 5)

**Click resolution (a click = press and release within `tapMaxDragPixels`, press not over UI per `PointerInput.IsOverUI`):**
1. Raycast `Layers.PickupMask` for an `ITappable` with `CanTap` (as `SquadController.OnTapped` does): call it, stop.

3. Else if the clicked hex holds a friendly unit (`UnitAt`), or the ground point is within 0.6 units of any friendly soldier: select that unit, or deselect if it is already selected.
4. Else if a unit is selected: `OrderMove`; selection is kept either way.
5. Else: nothing.
A press that moves farther than the threshold becomes a pan: forward the per-frame delta to `BattleCamera.Pan` until release, and do not treat the release as a click. Escape (Input System keyboard) disarms, or deselects when nothing is armed. `OrderRejected` plays the `Reject` sound and flashes the hex in the invalid color for 0.3 s. Everything is ignored while `army.InputEnabled` is false or `BattleManager.Instance.Over`.
