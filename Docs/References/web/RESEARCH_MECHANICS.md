## 1. Core loop, objectives, win/lose

- Attacks are asynchronous: the player fights AI-controlled defenses laid out by a real player's base; the defender need not be online. [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars ; https://mmohuts.com/?p=258711]
- Each base has two circular capture points; both must be captured to win. Maps are described as linear: capture a point, defend it briefly, capture the next, then win. [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars]
- Multiplayer.it agrees in general terms: hold capture points for a few seconds, then defend them against waves to win. (Note: "waves" is the only mention of waves; MMOs describes brief defense, not explicit waves.) [VERIFIED: https://multiplayer.it/recensioni/155206-tactile-wars-strategia-canaglia.html]
- Lose condition: the battle is lost when all of the player's units die. [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars]
- Most enemy squads carry a flag-bearer (also called captain/leader in other sources). Killing it creates a bubble the player can tap to instantly destroy that enemy squad. Advice: kill the flag-bearer first so the squad is removed with no casualties. [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars]. The App Store feature text (via search snippet) says "capture enemy captains with a tap" [VERIFIED: App Store search snippet, https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/tactile-wars/id892320294]. A search-sourced guide snippet says that after bubbling a leader you should not pop it immediately; that detail could not be traced to a fetched page [UNVERIFIED].
- A "Reinforcement" bar (top right) fills as squads are killed; when full the player can summon reinforcements. [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars]
- Battles are real-time, fast, arcade-style. [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars]
- Timers: Multiplayer.it says the game avoids timers and energy-based monetization [VERIFIED: multiplayer.it URL above]. MMOs also says no energy system. No in-battle countdown timer was documented. [UNVERIFIED for battle timer]
- Grades: LevelWinner says attacking your own base and winning with a grade of "DDD" (the highest grade it mentions) indicates a well-defended base. [VERIFIED: levelwinner guide 1]. The grading scale itself, and any star ratings, are [UNVERIFIED].
- Post-battle rewards: Gamebrain says winning attacks yields chests that require keys to open; daily missions give some keys and coins. [VERIFIED: https://gamebrain.co/game/tactile-wars]. Search snippets from user reviews say a key is needed whether you win or lose, and only about 3 keys a day come from daily missions [UNVERIFIED: review snippets, not traced to a fetched page]. This conflicts with Multiplayer.it/MMOs "no timers/energy", though keys may be a separate gating mechanic added in later versions (the Multiplayer.it review is of v1.3; "Key Pack" IAP exists in the later App Store listing). [INFERRED: App Store IAP list + Gamebrain; keys likely post-launch addition, not confirmed]
- Players can be invaded even after losing a battle. [VERIFIED: gamebrain URL] (meaning unclear).

## 2. Controls

- Tap to move units; draw shapes on screen to change formation (circle makes a circle, arrow makes an arrow). [VERIFIED: https://mmos.com/review/tactile-wars]
- App Store text: one touch draws formations (lines, circles, squares) to position troops against approaching enemies. [VERIFIED: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tactile-wars/id892320294 ; mirrored at https://game-solver.com/tactile-wars/]
- Multiplayer.it: "tap and go" ("tocca e vai"); units attack automatically once targets are in range; the squad reshapes into tight clusters, lines or circles. [VERIFIED: multiplayer.it URL]
- LevelWinner: draw shapes and lines to direct units; "Draw a straight line parallel to the mine" to move past mines; a small tight circle is suggested for minefields. [VERIFIED: levelwinner guide 1]. This suggests a drawn line influences movement path as well as formation [INFERRED: same source]; exact line-to-position mapping (do soldiers distribute along the drawn line, does the squad travel the path) is [UNVERIFIED].
